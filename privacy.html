Privacy Policy

Leyline Falls — puzzle game for Android · Last updated 17 November 2026

Leyline Falls keeps your progress on your device, and shows ads through Google AdMob. It has no user accounts and no servers of ours. The ad service collects some data to show and measure ads (listed below); you can pay once to remove the ads. Google Play handles that payment and we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Leyline Falls is developed and published by Ivan Fiszer as an independent developer. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What Leyline Falls stores, and where

Leyline Falls keeps the following in files on your device, readable only by the app itself:

  • Your progress: the resources you have gathered, the rooms and upgrades you have built, and your deepest mine floor
  • A game in progress, so it can be continued if the app closes
  • App settings such as tips, vibration and mine controls

None of this is sent to us. Leyline Falls has no server of its own. The only data that leaves your device is what the ad service collects, described in the next section.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Leyline Falls shows a full-screen ad after some runs, using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software on your device collects and shares with Google:

  • Your Android advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data of the ad software

This is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and security. Google handles it under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data and do not sell it.

Your choices.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Leyline Falls asks for your consent with Google's consent form when it first starts. You can change your answer later with the Privacy choices button in Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can remove all ads for good with the one-time Remove ads purchase.

Remove ads purchase

Remove ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. At each start the app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Remove ads. No other purchase data is collected by us.

What Leyline Falls does not do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no identity to verify.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own. We receive no usage statistics, no event logs, and no crash reports from your device.
  • No profile of you. We do not build a profile of you, and do not read your contacts, precise location, photos, files, messages, or call history.
  • No selling. We have no personal data of yours to sell.

Permissions Leyline Falls requests, and why

  • Vibration — for short buzzes during play. You can turn this off in the app's Settings.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to check your purchase with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID — used by the ad service, as described above.
  • Billing — to offer the Remove ads purchase through Google Play.

Leyline Falls requests no storage, precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, or notification permissions.

Your data, your control

  • Reset anything — Settings has a Reset progress button that starts a fresh game.
  • Delete everything — uninstalling Leyline Falls removes the app's files and all settings from your device. Because we hold no copy, uninstalling is a complete deletion. There is no account to close and no deletion request to file.
  • Android backup — if automatic app backup is turned on in your device's Google settings, Android may copy the app's local files to your own Google account and restore them when you reinstall. That is a feature of your device and your Google account; we do not receive these copies.

Children

Leyline Falls is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the ad settings are not set up for child-directed use.

Google Play

Leyline Falls is distributed through Google Play. Google collects its own data in connection with app distribution — downloads, crash diagnostics you opt into at the Android level — under Google's Privacy Policy, which is outside our control and not covered by this policy. Any aggregate statistics Google shows us in Play Console do not identify individual users.

If this policy changes

If a future version of Leyline Falls changes what it stores or collects, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
